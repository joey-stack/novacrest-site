An R of O (Right of Occupancy) is an allocation grant by the Minister of the Federal Capital Territory conferring legal rights to develop land. A C of O (Certificate of Occupancy) is the registered title deed issued once all statutory survey, town planning, and ground rent requirements are satisfied. All Novacrest properties have clear, registrable status without community or legal dispute.
