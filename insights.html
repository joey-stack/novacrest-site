Land Title Types in Abuja Explained: C of O vs. R of O vs. Excision

When investing in Abuja real estate from the diaspora, the single most critical safeguard is understanding the cadastral title. Abuja operates under the Federal Capital Development Authority (FCDA) and the Abuja Geographic Information Systems (AGIS).

1. Certificate of Occupancy (C of O): The highest statutory land title in the FCT. Granted directly under the hand of the Minister of the Federal Capital Territory for a 99-year leasehold. It affords absolute collateral validity and is registered directly in the AGIS central land registry database.

2. Right of Occupancy (R of O): An official statutory offer of allocation made by the FCDA or an Area Council. While legally valid, an R of O must subsequently be recertified and processed into a substantive C of O.

3. Government Excision & Gazette: Applies predominantly to developing corridors (such as Karshi and Kuje) where native ancestral lands have been legally partitioned and formally gazetted by the government for private residential development.
